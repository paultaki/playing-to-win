The choice cascade (Winning Aspiration → Where to Play → How to Win →
Required Capabilities → Management Systems) is the core conceptual
contribution of the book. This skill is an implementation that applies the
cascade. It does not reproduce the book's text, examples, or proprietary
materials.

## Scope of Implementation

This skill includes:

- **Operational instructions** for applying the cascade to a business idea,
  offer, or codebase.
- **Original calibration examples** in `references/strategy_example_bank.md`
  (written by the skill author, not drawn from the book).
- **Original positioning patterns** in `references/positioning_patterns.md`.
- **An execution lens** in `references/execution_patterns.md` based on the
  skill author's operator experience, not from the book.

This skill does **not** include:

- The book's text or excerpts beyond brief framework-naming citations.
- The book's case studies (P&G, Olay, etc.).
- The book's diagrams or proprietary visual material.
