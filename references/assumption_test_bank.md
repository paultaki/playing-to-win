# Assumption Test Bank

Use this file to create practical validation plans.

## Buyer Urgency Assumptions

**Assumption:** The buyer feels the problem urgently enough to act.

**How to test:**
- Ask 20 target buyers what they currently do about the problem
- Track whether they have spent money solving it before
- Look for time-sensitive triggers
- Run direct outreach with a specific offer and measure replies

**Weak signal:** "That sounds interesting."

**Strong signal:** "Can you help me with this now?"

## Willingness to Pay Assumptions

**Assumption:** The buyer will pay enough to support the business model.

**How to test:**
- Offer a paid manual version before building software
- Present 2 to 3 price points
- Ask for payment, deposit, or signed pilot
- Compare price to existing budget category

**Weak signal:** Positive feedback.

**Strong signal:** Payment, referral, signed pilot, or calendar commitment with decision-maker.

## Category Understanding Assumptions

**Assumption:** The buyer understands what this is quickly.

**How to test:**
- Show landing page headline to 10 target buyers
- Ask: "What do you think this does?"
- Ask: "Who is this for?"
- Ask: "Why would someone buy it?"
- Measure confusion before explaining

**Weak signal:** You need a long explanation.

**Strong signal:** Buyer explains the value back in their own words.

## Channel Assumptions

**Assumption:** The buyer can be reached through the chosen channel.

**How to test:**
- Build a list of 100 target prospects
- Send direct outreach
- Test LinkedIn, email, referral, paid search, or partnerships
- Measure qualified conversations, not vanity metrics

**Weak signal:** Impressions or likes.

**Strong signal:** Qualified replies and booked calls.

## Differentiation Assumptions

**Assumption:** The offer is meaningfully different from alternatives.

**How to test:**
- List direct and indirect alternatives
- Ask buyers what they would use if this did not exist
- Compare against current workaround
- Identify what makes switching worth it

**Weak signal:** Founder believes it is different.

**Strong signal:** Buyer says, "I haven't seen anyone explain it that way."

## Fulfillment Assumptions

**Assumption:** The offer can be delivered repeatedly without killing margin.

**How to test:**
- Manually fulfill 3 to 5 paid versions
- Track hours per delivery
- Track revisions and support burden
- Identify repeatable templates
- Estimate gross margin

**Weak signal:** Founder can do it once with heroic effort.

**Strong signal:** Process improves after each delivery.

## Trust Assumptions

**Assumption:** The buyer trusts the provider enough to buy.

**How to test:**
- Run calls with cold prospects
- Test proof assets
- Use case studies or sample audits
- Track objections around credibility

**Weak signal:** They like the idea but delay.

**Strong signal:** They ask about timeline, price, and next steps.

## Timing Assumptions

**Assumption:** There is a trigger that makes the buyer care now.

**Possible triggers:**
- Budget review
- Renewal date
- Compliance issue
- Reputation risk
- Family health event
- Competitive loss
- New regulation
- Major life event
- New technology shift
- Public embarrassment

**How to test:**
- Ask what happened right before they searched for a solution
- Build offers around the trigger
- Compare triggered buyers to general buyers

**Strong signal:** The buyer has a deadline or recent pain.
